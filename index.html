Founders are made at the table, not found on a stage.

The Founder Mafia is a long-form podcast and a family of founders building in India.

Every episode is one founder at one table, telling the story the pitch deck leaves out: the first hires, the money that almost didn’t come, the product that had to die.

Around the show sits the family — listeners who come to live recordings, founders who return as mentors, and guests who keep helping the next guest.

    How a sit-down works

    Four steps from a founder’s first message to the episode going out.

    1. The pitch

      A founder pitches their story, or we invite them after following their work.

    2. The pre-call

      Twenty minutes to find the moments worth an hour. No script is shared.

    3. The recording

      One take, two microphones. The guest can pull anything off the record afterwards.

    4. The episode

      Out on YouTube and Spotify, with one lesson for the next founder.
