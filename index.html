Every episode is a sit-down

One table, two microphones, no slides. We talk about the first hires, the money that almost didn’t come, the product that had to be shut down — the decisions that actually built the company.

Who sits down
Founders building in India, from first cheque to scale.
The format
Long-form, one conversation, no script handed to the guest.
What you leave with
The playbook, in the founder’s own words.

The family deck

Every guest gets a card. Turn one over to see who sat down and what they gave away.

House rules at the table

  • Nothing is off the record unless you say so.
  • Numbers, not adjectives.
  • The failures get as much airtime as the wins.
  • Every guest leaves one lesson behind for the next founder.
