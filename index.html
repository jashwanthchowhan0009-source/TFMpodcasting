What this is

From the mic to the market

The Founder Mafia is a long-form podcast and a family of founders building in India.

Every episode is one founder at one table, telling the story the pitch deck leaves out: the first hires, the money that almost didn't come, the product that had to die. Around the show sits the family — founders, operators and investors who meet, swap notes and back each other.

  • StoriesFounders tell it straight, on the record.
  • CapitalInvestors at the table explain how cheques really get written.
  • OperatorsPeople who have scaled teams share what broke first.
  • CommunityListeners become members, members become guests.
