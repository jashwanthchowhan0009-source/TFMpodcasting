About us

From the mic to the market

The Founder Mafia is a long-form podcast and a family of founders building in India.

Every episode is one founder at one table, telling the story the pitch deck leaves out: the first hires, the money that almost didn’t come, the product that had to die.

Around the show sits the family — listeners who come to live recordings, founders who return as mentors, and guests who keep helping the next guest.
