Profile

A table where founders tell it straight.

The Founder Mafia is a long-form podcast for people building companies in India. Each episode is one founder, one table and one honest conversation about how the company really got built.

No highlight reels. We ask about the cheque that bounced, the co-founder who left and the pivot nobody saw coming, because that is the part the next founder can actually use.

Around the show sits the family: listeners who come to live recordings, founders who return as mentors, and guests who keep helping the next guest.

Your host

Phanindra Aarav

Phanindra Aarav runs the table.

He asks the questions founders usually only get asked off-mic — about cash in the bank, co-founder fights and the month they nearly quit — and keeps asking until the real answer shows up.

The set

Two chairs. Four microphones. No script.

Every sit-down is recorded in one take, and the guest can take anything off the record afterwards.

How a sit-down works

  1. The pitch

    A founder pitches their story, or we invite them after following their work.

  2. The pre-call

    Twenty minutes to find the moments worth an hour. No script is shared.

  3. The recording

    One take, two microphones. The guest can pull anything off the record afterwards.

  4. The episode

    It goes out on YouTube and Spotify with the guest's one lesson for the next founder.

House rules

  • Nothing is off the record unless you say so.
  • Numbers, not adjectives.
  • The failures get as much airtime as the wins.
  • Every guest leaves one lesson for the next founder.
